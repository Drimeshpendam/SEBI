# Daily Revision — Nov 13, 2026
> Today's focus: Paper 1 speed — triage order, kill rules, reflex drills

## The Exam Math (why you don't need 10/7min)
- 80 Qs / 60 min ≈ 45s avg, but GA+English are recall (~20s) → **~33 min for 40 Quant+Reasoning Qs**
- Target is **8–12 attempts per section at ≥85%** — marks-per-minute, not questions-per-minute
- A 4–5-min puzzle set = 4–5 marks = **on pace**, not slow

## The 3 Speed Leaks (fix in order)
1. **No triage** — never solve Q1→Q20 straight. Scan 60–90s first, tag instant-kill vs slow.
2. **No kill rule** — 15s to find the method or flag it; **50–75s hard cap** per single Q; puzzle/DI sets cap at 5–6 min.
3. **Foundation not reflex** — fractions/squares/multiply must be automatic or every Q costs 3 min.

## Question Speed Buckets
- **Instant-kill (<40s):** approximation · quadratic sign-trick (read signs only) · alphanumeric/word series · blood-relation tree · direction · inequality
- **Medium (60–90s):** wrong-number series · easy arithmetic · syllogism
- **Slow (last):** DI sets · seating/puzzles — pick clean table/line over caselet

## Reasoning Speed Protocol
- **Singles first (~20–40s each, ~4–5 min total):** alphanumeric series → blood relations → direction → inequality → syllogism → stmt-assumption → data sufficiency
- **Then puzzles, easiest first:** linear/month > box/floor > circular > **mixed-facing LAST**
- Only need **8–12 attempts** — pick 2–3 easiest sets, skip the monster

## Where Puzzle Time Leaks
- Restarts kill time — **two-case rule** (branch, don't guess) prevents every restart
- Re-reading kills time — classify each clue in 5s: **anchor / relative / glued / order-only / negative**
- **6-min cap:** 2 unresolved cases → take sure answers, move on

## Clue-Reading Reflex (drill until instant)
| Clue says | Means | Act |
|---|---|---|
| "3rd from left" | **anchor** | place now |
| "n between A,B" | relative, \|diff\|=n+1 | branch both cases |
| "immediately above/left" | **glued block** | move as one unit |
| "somewhere above/after" | **order only, NOT glued** | #1 misread trap |
| "not at end/Mon" | **negative** | last — prunes leftovers |
| south-facing/circular | **mirror** | arrows before placing |
