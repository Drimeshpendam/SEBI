## Motivation set

**"Why SEBI? Why not stay in private IT?"**
> Angle: public markets + tech is a rare intersection; regulator work has outsized
> impact vs one company's bottom line. Never frame as "job security" alone.

**"Why the IT stream and not General?"**
> Angle: your CS/engineering background is the qualifier AND the value-add — SEBI's
> work is increasingly a technology problem (surveillance, CSCRF, settlement infra).

**"Where do you see yourself in 5 years?"**
> Angle: Grade B/C trajectory inside SEBI — deeper into market-tech/investor-tech,
> not "your own startup."

## Situational set (these come as hypotheticals)

**"You discover a colleague leaking deal info. Walk me through it."**

## Trap questions

- "Your marks in X semester were low — why?" → own it, one line, pivot to evidence since.
- "Are you preparing for other exams?" → honest + framed (SEBI is the target).
- "You could earn more in private — really?" → acknowledge; impact/stability/mission is the trade you chose.
