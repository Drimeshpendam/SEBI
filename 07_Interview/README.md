# Interview Prep (15% of final merit)

Final merit = Phase 2 TWS (85%) + Interview (15%). Small weight — but the margin
between shortlisted and selected is usually built here, and it's the cheapest
marks to bank early.

## The drip protocol — Sunday, 20 min, non-negotiable from Day 1

Interview prep is a weekly habit, not a post-Mains scramble:

| Week rhythm | Task |
|---|---|
| Odd Sundays | One SEBI-knowledge card from `SEBI_Knowledge_Base.md` — read, then say a 90-second spoken answer out loud ("What does SEBI do about X?") |
| Even Sundays | Draft/refine ONE answer in `HR_Answer_Bank.md` — write it, speak it, trim it to ~60–90 seconds |

By Day 49 that produces ~7 spoken SEBI answers + ~7 polished HR answers — enough
that the interview ramp in Jan is *polishing*, not starting from zero.

## Files

| File | What's in it |
|---|---|
| `SEBI_Knowledge_Base.md` | The facts panels ask about — structure, powers, recent regulations, tech initiatives |
| `HR_Answer_Bank.md` | Classic questions + YOUR drafted answers (edit these, they're yours) |
| `IT_Interview_Topics.md` | Tech-flavored questions for the IT stream + answer angles |
| `DAF_Worksheet.md` | Every DAF entry → the 3 questions it can generate |

## Rules

- **Speak answers, don't just write them.** The deliverable is a 60–90s spoken answer.
- Interviewers ask from YOUR DAF — every claim in it needs a story behind it.
- Jan ramp (post-Phase-1): 2 mock interviews/week + this folder daily.
