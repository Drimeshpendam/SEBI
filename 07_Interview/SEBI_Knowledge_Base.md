## Identity card

- **SEBI** — Securities and Exchange Board of India
- Established **1988** (non-statutory) → statutory powers via **SEBI Act, 1992**
- HQ: **Mumbai** (Bandra Kurla Complex); regional offices: New Delhi, Kolkata, Chennai, Ahmedabad
- Reports to **Ministry of Finance**; governed by a Board — Chair + members from RBI, GoI, and appointed experts
- **Chairman:** Tuhin Kanta Pandey (took charge Mar 2025, succeeded Madhabi Puri Buch) — *re-verify at interview time*
- Core mandate triad: **protect investors · develop the market · regulate it**

## What SEBI actually regulates (answer fodder)

- Stock exchanges (NSE, BSE), brokers, merchant bankers, mutual funds/AMCs
- FPIs, AIFs, REITs/InvITs, credit rating agencies, depositories (NSDL/CDSL — via Depositories Act 1996)
- IPOs/primary markets, insider trading (PIT Regs 2015), takeover code (SAST 2011), LODR (listing obligations)
- Derivatives (F&O), algo trading, market intermediaries — and **fraud/surveillance/enforcement**

## Tech-relevant initiatives (your stream's angle)

- **T+1 settlement** (full rollout 2023) → **T+0 beta** pilot (2024) — India among first globally
- **SCORES 2.0** — investor grievance portal (web + app)
- **Saa₹thi** — investor education mobile app
- **CSCRF** — Cybersecurity & Cyber Resilience Framework for regulated entities (2024) — audit, incident reporting, SOC requirements
- Surveillance & analytics — SEBI's in-house data warehousing (DWBISS project historically), AI/ML for surveillance, social-media monitoring pilots
- **Regulatory sandbox** for market-tech innovation; algo/HFT regulation consultation (2025)
- Digital KYC/e-KYC enablement, online bond platforms framework, DDP (demat of everything)
