# Practice 100 Campaign — 100 questions per CONCEPT

Each concept below gets up to 100 questions, in sets of 10. Every set: timed,
A/B/C tagged, wrongs → `Error_Log.md`, audit row → `Practice_Log.md`.

**Graduation rule:** a concept retires early at ≥90% accuracy across 2 consecutive
sets (min 40 Qs) — no need to grind what's already reflex. High-weightage concepts
(marked ⭐) run the full 100 regardless.

**Order:** follow the study plan's week order so practice doubles as revision.
DBMS first (Week 1's subject — its volume IS this week's topic test).
