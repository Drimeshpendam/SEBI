# SEBI Grade A — Exam Pattern (IT Stream)

Based on recent notifications. Always re-verify against the official SEBI notification when it drops (expected ~mid-2027).

## Stage 1 — Phase 1 (Screening)

Two online papers, same day. Marks NOT counted in final selection — qualifying only.

| Paper | Contents | Marks | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paper 1 | General Awareness (incl. financial sector), English, Quant, Reasoning | 100 | 4 sections, need ~30% in EACH section + ~40% aggregate |
| Paper 2 | IT stream subjects (MCQ) | 100 | Aggregate cutoff applies across both papers |

- Negative marking: 1/4 mark per wrong answer.
- Key trap: Paper 1 has **sectional cutoffs** — a weak section fails you even with a high total.

## Stage 2 — Phase 2 (Mains)

| Paper | Contents | Marks | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paper 1 | English (Descriptive): essay, precis, comprehension | 100 | 1/3 |
| Paper 2 | IT stream — deeper coverage than Phase 1 | 100 | 2/3 |

- Descriptive paper is typed on screen — practice typing speed AND structured writing.
- Phase 2 marks count fully toward merit.

## Stage 3 — Interview

- Final merit: Phase 2 (85%) + Interview (15%).

## Implications for prep

1. Phase 1 Paper 1 is pure screening — get comfortably above cutoff, don't chase 95.
2. Phase 2 Paper 2 is where merit is decided → IT depth matters most.
3. Descriptive English is 1/3 of merit and the most-neglected paper — start early.
4. Sectional cutoffs → no subject can be left at zero.
