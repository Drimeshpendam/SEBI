## Sample Questions — the actual recall format SEBI uses

GA isn't solved, it's recalled — but seeing the exact formats trains what to notice while reading news. These mirror real paper styles:

**Ex 1 — SEBI structure.** "SEBI was given statutory powers in which year? (a) 1988 (b) 1992 (c) 1995 (d) 2000"
**Ans: 1992** (established 1988, statutory 1992 — the two dates get swapped as distractors. This is THE most-asked SEBI fact.)

**Ex 2 — regulator jurisdiction.** "Which entity regulates collective investment schemes in India?"
**Ans: SEBI** — and the flip: insurance→IRDAI, banks→RBI, pension→PFRDA, telecom→TRAI. Regulator↔domain is a guaranteed 1–2 questions.

**Ex 3 — market instrument.** "A company raising capital from public for the first time issues a/an ___."
**Ans: IPO.** Variants: FPO (further offer), rights issue (existing shareholders), QIP (institutional), OFS (offer for sale).

**Ex 4 — SEBI terminology.** "Green Shoe option in an IPO refers to ___."
**Ans: over-allotment option** — lets underwriters sell extra shares to stabilize post-listing price.

**Ex 5 — RBI/monetary.** "Repo rate is the rate at which ___."
**Ans: RBI lends to commercial banks** (reverse repo = RBI borrows FROM banks). Repo↑ = tighter money; the direction question recurs.

**Ex 6 — scheme in news.** "PM-JDY relates to ___." / "Stand-Up India targets ___."
**Ans: financial inclusion (bank accounts) / loans to SC-ST & women entrepreneurs.** Budget + flagship schemes = 2–3 questions/paper.

**Ex 7 — index/report.** "India's rank in the latest [Ease of Doing Business–style] report / which body publishes Financial Stability Report?"
**Ans: know the publisher, not just the rank** — FSR = RBI (half-yearly). WEF=World Economic Forum, HDI=UNDP. Publisher is the safer bet.

**Ex 8 — appointment.** "Who is the current SEBI Chairperson / RBI Governor / Finance Minister?"
**Ans: the three names change — maintain in the monthly log.** Appointment questions always use options who held the post 1–2 terms back.

**Ex 9 — static GK (the long tail).** "Headquarters of SEBI / BSE location / currency of Japan."
**Ans: Mumbai / Mumbai / Yen.** HQ questions cluster around: SEBI Mumbai, RBI Mumbai, IRDAI Hyderabad, NABARD Mumbai.

**Ex 10 — current-affairs-in-depth.** "Recent SEBI action: the 2024–25 rules on finfluencers / F&O curbs were aimed at ___."
**Ans: protecting retail investors from unregistered advice / speculative derivative losses.** SEBI's own news IS the syllabus — its press releases are the highest-yield GA source for this exam.

### How to use
When you read daily affairs, mentally convert each item into one of these 10 formats ("who/which/year/HQ/purpose?"). That conversion is the whole GA skill — you're not reading news, you're collecting future MCQs.
