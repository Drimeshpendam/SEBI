# Financial Awareness — SEBI & Capital Markets (exam-critical GA)

## SEBI Essentials (memorize verbatim)
- Established: April 12, **1988** (non-statutory); statutory power via **SEBI Act, 1992**. HQ: Mumbai. Regional offices: Ahmedabad, Kolkata, Chennai, Delhi (+ local offices).
- Chairman: appointed by Govt of India — check current name near exam (changes matter). 8 members on board incl. RBI & Finance Ministry nominees.
- Regulates: securities market, stock exchanges, brokers, merchant bankers, mutual funds, FPIs, credit rating agencies, depositories participants.
- Objectives: protect investor interests, develop + regulate securities market.
- Does NOT regulate: commodity derivatives → that's also SEBI since 2015 (FMC merged into SEBI). Insurance = IRDAI, pensions = PFRDA, banking = RBI, companies = MCA.

## Key laws & concepts
- SEBI Act 1992, SCRA 1956 (securities contracts), Depositories Act 1996, Companies Act 2013, PMLA 2002, insider trading regs (PIT 2015), LODR (listing obligations & disclosure requirements).
- Insider trading: trading on unpublished price-sensitive info — SEBI's signature enforcement area.
- Circuit breakers: index-wide at 10/15/20% Nifty/Sensex moves; stock-level price bands (2/5/10/20%).
- IPO: primary market; DRHP → RHP; book building vs fixed price; SEBI vets offer documents (not price).
- Mutual funds: pool money, NAV = (assets−liabilities)/units; SEBI MF regs 1996; SIP; categories equity/debt/hybrid/ELSS. ELSS = tax-saving, 3-yr lock-in.
- AIFs (I/II/III), REITs & InvITs (listed trusts — real estate / infrastructure).
- FPI (foreign portfolio investors), FDI vs FPI distinction (FPI = passive, <10% typically).

## Market infrastructure
- Exchanges: NSE (1994, Nifty 50 since 1996), BSE (1875 — Asia's oldest, Sensex 1986 base 1978-79=100).
- Depositories: NSDL (1996), CDSL (1999) — demat holds securities; SEBI registers them.
- Clearing: NCL (NSE), ICCL (BSE) — counterparty guarantee.
- Trading terms: T+1 settlement (India fully since Jan 2023 — ahead of most markets; T+0 optional rolling out), demat, short selling, margin, F&O derivatives, index vs stock F&O, expiry days.
- Benchmarks: Sensex = 30 stocks (free-float mcap), Nifty = 50; sectoral: Nifty Bank, IT.
- Market cap, free float, PE ratio, dividend yield — definitional MCQs.
