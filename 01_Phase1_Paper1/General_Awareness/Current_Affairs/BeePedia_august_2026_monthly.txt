RBI MPC Keeps Repo Rate Unchanged at 5.25%, Maintains Neutral Stance 
Key Points: 
• Repo Rate Unchanged: The Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) voted unanimously to 
keep the policy repo rate unchanged at 5.25% for the 62nd meeting held from August 3 -
5, 2026. The SDF rate remains at 5.00% and MSF rate at 5.50%. 
• Neutral Stance Retained: The MPC decided to continue with a neutral stance, allowing 
flexibility to respond to evolving macroeconomic developments. 
• GDP Growth Projection: Real GDP growth for 2026 -27 is projected at 6.7%, with Q1 at 
7.0%, Q2 at 6.4%, Q3 at 6.5%, and Q4 at 6.8%. 
• Inflation Projection: CPI inflation for 2026 -27 is projected at 5.0%. It is expected to peak 
in Q3 at 5.9% before moderating. Core inflation (excluding food and fuel) remained 
unchanged at 3.9% during May-June. 
• Reasons for Pause: The MPC noted that headline inflation is rising primarily due to supply-
side pressures from food and fuel, but it is not getting broad -based as core inflation 
remains moderate. The MPC wants greater clarity on inflation, monsoon, and geopolitical 
uncertainties before any policy action. 
Bloomberg Defers Inclusion of Indian Government Bonds in Global Index Again 
Key Points: 
• Decision Delayed: Bloomberg Index Services has once again postponed the inclusion of 
Indian government securities (FAR bonds) into its flagship Bloomberg Global Aggregate 
Index. The decision will be reviewed again later. 
• Reason for Deferral: The index provider stated that while India has made positive market 
reforms, foreign investors want more time to see these changes work smoothly in daily 
trading and operations before they agree to inclusion. 
• India’s Reforms: To boost its chances, the Indian government recently exempted eligible 
foreign investors from taxes on interest income and capital gains on specified bonds. The 
RBI also allowed more bond categories under the FAR route. 
• Market Impact: The deferral is expected to cause a small rise in the benchmark 10 -year 
government bond yield (by about 4 -5 basis points), as the market had already priced in 
the inclusion. 
• Key Operational Concerns: Foreign investors still have concerns about the smooth 
functioning of electronic trading platforms, settlement processes, and the complex KYC 
(Know Your Customer) process for onboarding new investors.

CD Issuances Plunge in July as FCNR(B) Deposits Surge Under RBI Swap Scheme 
Key Points: 
• Sharp Decline: Banks raised around ₹95,000 crore through Certificates of Deposit (CDs) 
in July, a significant drop from ₹1.8 trillion in June, as alternative funding sources became 
available. 
• FCNR(B) Surge: Following RBI's concessional swap facility announced in early June, 
banks have mobilised nearly $41 billion in foreign exchange inflows, including $36.7 billion 
through fresh FCNR(B) deposits as of July 31. 
• Lower Funding Costs: The increased liquidity from FCNR(B) deposits has reduced banks' 
dependence on CDs, leading to a softening of CD rates by up to 70 basis points from 
7.57% to 6.85%. 
• Why This Matters: Banks use CDs to manage short-term liquidity and bridge asset-liability 
mismatches. The sharp rise in FCNR(B) deposits has provided a cheaper and more stable 
source of wholesale funding. 
• Future Outlook: Experts believe the trend may continue if banks accumulate sufficient 
FCNR(B) deposits before the September deadline. However, a sudden rise in credit 
demand could force banks to return to the CD market. 
 
Supreme Court Directs RBI to Frame Uniform SOP for Banks on Cyber Fraud Cases 
Key Points: 
• SOP for Banks: The Supreme Court has directed RBI to frame a standard operating 
procedure (SOP) for banks to handle cyber fraud cases, including 'digital arrests' and mule 
accounts, within four weeks. 
• Key SOP Components: The SOP will cover temporary hold on debits from suspected 
accounts, grievance redressal mechanism, restoration of defrauded money, and 
measures to create public awareness. 
• Action Against Digital Arrest Scams: The Court directed the Centre, states, and other 
authorities to strengthen measures against the rise in digital arrest scams, noting that the 
existing system requires wider implementation and quicker action. 
• Victim Compensation Framework: The Court ordered the formation of an 
interdepartmental committee to examine a framework for victim compensation and shared 
liability for losses from cyber frauds. 
• Operational Timelines: States and Union Territories have been directed to operationalise 
State Cyber Crime Coordination Centres and implement the e-Zero FIR system within four 
weeks.

Tata Sons Continues as Upper-Layer NBFC; RBI Says Deregistration Pending 
Key Points: 
• Status Unchanged: RBI Governor Sanjay Malhotra indicated that Tata Sons continues to 
be classified as an upper -layer non -banking financial company (NBFC) under the 
principle-based regulatory framework. 
• Deregistration Pending: Tata Sons' application to surrender its NBFC -Core Investment 
Company (CIC) licence and deregister remains under consideration by the RBI. 
• Upper-Layer Criteria: Under revised norms, NBFCs with assets exceeding ₹1 trillion 
qualify for the upper layer. Tata Sons' standalone assets are over ₹1.75 trillion. 
• Listing Requirement: Upper-layer NBFCs are subject to enhanced regulation and must list 
on stock exchanges within three years of being classified. Tata Sons was first classified 
as an upper-layer NBFC in 2022. 
• Key Issue: Although Tata Sons has become debt-free, the RBI may still consider it to have 
indirect access to public funds through its group companies, which could require it to 
remain registered and comply with mandatory listing requirements. 
 
RBI Proposes On-Tap Licensing for Urban Co-operative Banks After 22 Years 
Key Points: 
• Licensing Resumes: RBI has issued draft guidelines for on -tap licensing of urban co -
operative banks (UCBs), marking the resumption of UCB licensing after nearly 22 years. 
• Eligibility Criteria: Credit co-operative societies seeking conversion into UCBs must have 
a minimum deposit base of ₹10,000 crore, net worth of at least ₹300 crore, and at least 
10 years of existence. 
• Financial Health Requirements: Applicants must maintain a Capital -to-Risk Weighted 
Assets Ratio (CRAR) of at least 12% and a net Non -Performing Asset (NPA) ratio of not 
more than 3%. 
• Application Process: Applications will be accepted on-tap through RBI's PRAVAAH portal. 
Approval requires at least two -thirds shareholder consent and a No-Objection Certificate 
from the Central Registrar. 
• Approval Timeline: In -principle approval will remain valid for 18 months. Rejected 
applicants will not be eligible to apply for another banking licence for three years. 
 
RBI Eases PSL Calculation for Bank Loans Backed by Non-Resident Deposits 
Key Points:

• Regulatory Relief: RBI has allowed banks to exclude advances backed by fresh non -
resident deposits from the calculation of Adjusted Net Bank Credit (ANBC), which is used 
to determine Priority Sector Lending (PSL) targets. 
• Eligible Deposits: Advances backed by fresh FCNR(B) deposits (tenure of 3 -5 years) 
mobilised between June 8, 2026 and September 30, 2026, and NRE term deposits (tenure 
of 3 years or more) mobilised between June 19, 2026 and September 30, 2026, will be 
excluded. 
• Purpose of Relief: The move reduces the overall PSL compliance burden for banks that 
are actively mobilising non-resident capital during the specified window. 
• Context: In June 2026, RBI introduced a swap facility for fresh FCNR(B) funds to 
incentivise capital inflows and strengthen India's balance of payments position. 
• PSL Requirement: Commercial banks are typically required to deploy 40% of their ANBC 
towards priority sectors like agriculture, micro-enterprises, and weaker sections. This relief 
effectively decouples these specific advances from mandatory PSL obligations. 
 
RBI Proposes New Derivative-Risk Capital Framework Effective April 2027 
Key Points: 
• New Framework Proposed: RBI has released a draft framework for capital charge on 
derivative trades, replacing the existing rules from 2011. It will apply to all commercial 
banks except small finance banks, payments banks, and local area banks. 
• Credit Valuation Adjustment (CVA): The framework focuses on CVA, which is an 
adjustment banks make to derivative prices to account for the risk that the counterparty 
may default. Banks must set aside capital to cover such potential losses. 
• Alignment with Global Standards: The move aligns Indian regulations with the revised 
Basel III guidelines issued by the Basel Committee on Banking Supervision (BCBS). 
• Simpler Treatment for Smaller Banks: Banks with derivatives book notional amount of ₹10 
trillion or less can skip the CVA computation and instead set their CVA capital requirement 
equal to 100% of their counterparty credit risk capital requirement. 
• Implementation Timeline: The draft rules will take effect from April 1, 2027. RBI has invited 
comments from stakeholders until August 28, 2026. 
 
RBI Proposes to Harmonise Framework on Interest Rates on Advances 
Key Points: 
• Standardisation Move: RBI has proposed to harmonise and standardise the regulatory 
framework governing interest rates on advances across all regulated entities (REs) to 
enhance transparency in loan pricing.

7 
 
• No Major Changes: RBI Governor Sanjay Malhotra clarified that the proposal does not 
bring major changes to the existing lending rate framework and will not require NBFCs to 
shift to the External Benchmark-Linked Lending Rate (EBLR) regime. 
• Key Objectives: The rationalisation seeks to address operational issues in the MCLR and 
EBLR frameworks, standardise divergent market practices on interest charging, and 
improve monetary policy transmission. 
• Consumer Protection: The measures aim to ensure greater uniformity, improve 
transparency in loan pricing, and strengthen consumer protection by helping borrowers 
understand how interest rates are set. 
• Current Lending Rate Landscape: Banks have evolved from Prime Lending Rate (until 
2010) to Base Rate (2010-2016), then to MCLR (from 2016), and finally EBLR (from 2019). 
Currently, 67.6% of bank loans are linked to EBLR. 
 
 
India-Maldives Launch Favara-UPI Cross-Border Payment Corridor 
Key Points: 
• Corridor Operationalised: The Maldives Monetary Authority (MMA) has operationalised 
the integration of Favara (Maldives' Instant Payment System) with India's Unified 
Payments Interface (UPI), enabling real -time cross -border transfers between the two 
countries. 
• Transaction Process: Transactions are initiated in Maldivian Rufiyaa (MVR) and credited 
directly in Indian Rupees (INR) to UPI-enabled bank accounts in India. 
• Initial Coverage: The service initially covers customers of Bank of Maldives Plc and 
Maldives Islamic Bank Plc through the Favara payment platform. 
• Permitted Remittances: The service supports family maintenance -related remittances 
(both inward and outward) and gift -related remittances under Foreign Outward 
Remittance. 
• Future Expansion: Subsequent phases will introduce QR code-based merchant payments 
and other digital payment solutions to strengthen India-Maldives financial connectivity.

concentrated effects on struggling sectors like wood products and kitchen cabinet makers.

Uttar Pradesh and Bihar's Per Capita Income Still Trails India's Average 
• Despite India's per capita income rising more than 40 times over the past 35 years, Uttar 
Pradesh and Bihar have consistently remained among the poorest states, with their per 
capita income still trailing the national average. 
• Bihar's per capita income has fallen sharply relative to the national average, dropping from 
53% in 1990 -91 to about 34% in recent years. UP held  three-fourths of the national 
average in 1990-91; that share has since slipped to about half. 
• Maharashtra, once among the top five states at the time of reforms, has dropped out 
despite being home to India's financial capital Mumbai —largely due to the "law of large 
numbers" where larger states grow slower, and faster population growth. 
• Punjab has also slipped out of the top five since 2010 -11 due to heavy reliance on 
agriculture, insurgency -related business insecurity, floods, and shocks from 
demonetisation and the pandemic. 
• Only two states —Delhi and Goa —have consistently remained among the top five 
throughout the post-reform period. Karnataka is the latest entrant to the top five in 2024 -
25. 
• There has been slight improvement in Bihar, Jharkhand, and UP's per capita income 
relative to the national average between 2020 -21 and 2024-25, possibly linked to GST's 
destination-based structure, though experts caution this is not conclusive evidence of 
structural narrowing of regional disparities.

• CSIR-Institute of Himalayan Bioresource Technology (CSIR -IHBT) discovered a new 
sunflower-family species, ‘Doronicum sanctum’, in Tawang district, Arunachal Pradesh. 
• The species was discovered at an altitude of around 4,000 metres through botanical 
surveys and laboratory analysis. 
• The findings were published in an international peer-reviewed journal by Springer Nature. 
• This is the first documentation of the Doronicum genus in Northeast India; earlier Indian 
records were limited to the western Himalayas. 
• The species is commonly known as Leopard’s bane and was named sanctum because its 
type locality is near the Chumi Gyatse Holy Waterfalls. 
DoT & C-DOT Sign 5-Year Pact for Optical Network Testbed 
• The Department of Telecommunications (DoT) signed a 5-year agreement with the Centre 
for Development of Telematics (C -DOT) to establish an End -to-End Optical Network 
Testbed at C-DOT’s New Delhi campus. 
• The testbed will provide a real -world platform to test, validate and commercialise 
indigenous optical technologies. 
• It will integrate optical core, optical access and quantum security technologies, along with 
third-party testing tools. 
• The project will have two phases: the first 2 years for infrastructure setup and rollout, 
followed by 3 years of operations, maintenance, technical support and outreach. 
• It will also function as a training platform for advanced optical networking and help address 
telecom-sector skill gaps.

Scientists Discover First Subterranean Fish ‘Gangaichthys indonepalicus’ in Indo -
Gangetic Basin 
• Scientists including researchers from the Zoological Survey of India (ZSI) and Thackeray 
Wildlife Foundation reported the new subterranean fish species ‘Gangaichthys 
indonepalicus’ from Pashchim Champaran, Bihar, near the Nepal border. 
• It is the first recorded subterranean fish from the Gangetic Basin and Indo-Gangetic Plain. 
• The findings were published in the scientific journal ZooKeys by researchers from India 
and China. 
• The fish shows adaptations to underground environments, including complete lack of 
pigmentation, reduced skin-covered eyes and an elongated body. 
• It belongs to the Chaudhuriidae (earthworm eels) family under the Synbranchiformes order 
and represents a new genus and species, pending further genetic validation.

Arindam Chaudhuri Launches ‘Africa Before America’ 
• Management thinker, author and education entrepreneur Dr Arindam Chaudhuri launched 
his book ‘Africa Before America’ in August 2026. 
• The book examines African history, civilisations, global economic development and 
Africa’s significance before the rise of modern America. 
• It advocates reducing global income inequalities, promoting economic growth and human 
development in Africa, and creating a more equitable world order. 
• The book presents the “1.25:1 Egalitarian Concept”, based on Dr Malay Chaudhuri’s 
theory, proposing that the highest -paid employee should earn no more than 1.25 times 
the salary of the lowest-paid employee. 
• His notable works include The Great Indian Dream, Beyond God & Capitalism, What Marx 
Left Unsaid and 2047: A Roadmap to Beating USA & China. 
VP C. P. Radhakrishnan Releases ‘Sankalp Se Siddhi Ka Modi Yug’ 
• Vice President C. P. Radhakrishnan released ‘Sankalp Se Siddhi Ka Modi Yug’, authored 
by Ghanshyam Tiwari, Rajya Sabha MP. 
• The book was released at Uprashtrapati Bhavan, New Delhi, and published by Rupa 
Publications. 
• The author portrays Prime Minister Narendra Modi as “Setu Purush”, a bridge connecting 
the visions of national thinkers with their implementation in contemporary India. 
• The book examines India’s transformation over the past 12 years and the vision of Viksit 
Bharat @ 2047. 
• Key themes include Rashtra Pratham, Integral Humanism, Antyodaya, national integrity, 
social and economic justice, women -led development, self -reliance and transparent 
governance.

Anahat Singh Becomes First Indian to Win World Junior Squash Championship 
• 18-year-old Anahat Singh became the first Indian to win the women’s singles title at the 
World Junior Squash Championships 2026. 
• She defeated Egypt’s Ruqayya Salem 3–0 (11–3, 11–7, 11–9) in the final at Niagara-on-
the-Lake, Canada.

Chinappa’s 2005 runner-up finish as India’s best result.
